# Image Generation Prompt — Day 1

*12 March 2026 — Arc 1: First Arrival, Strip 3 / Arc 3: The Windowsill, Strip 1*

---

## Scene

A small, fluffy young owl — Konsti — has just landed on the wide windowsill of the Bauwagen. This is the moment of arrival: he has found his spot.

The view is from outside, looking slightly upward at the Bauwagen's large glass window. Konsti sits on the exterior windowsill, his body facing partly toward the viewer, his head turned to gaze out across the Schrottplatz. He has just settled — his talons are gripping the sill with the careful deliberation of a first landing, his body still slightly upright as if the act of sitting hasn't fully relaxed into staying yet. But his eyes are wide and soft, not startled — they have that particular quality of recognition: *oh. here.*

His ear tufts are slightly raised — alert but not alarmed. Curious. Taking it in. His feathers are fluffy and round, ruffled slightly by the southwest wind. He is small against the large window behind him.

The window glass behind Konsti reflects the grey-white sky and the dark silhouettes of bare trees. Condensation fogs the lower edges and corners of the glass. Through the glass, the dim interior of the Bauwagen is faintly visible — shadowy shapes of shelves, a table, the suggestion of tools and clutter. The windowsill itself is worn wood, slightly damp from the morning's rain, with a few drops still beading on its surface.

## Environment

The Bauwagen sits on the Schrottplatz in Rodgau on an early March afternoon. The sky is partly cloudy — high cloud cover with breaks where pale blue shows through. The light is soft and diffused, neither fully grey nor fully bright: the quality of light you get when the sun is there but behind a thin veil.

Around the Bauwagen: the scrapyard spreads out. In the middle ground, a pile of rusted metal — old car parts, a hubcap catching diffused light, stacked wooden pallets. To the left, a row of bare deciduous trees with dark skeletal branches against the sky, buds just barely swelling at the tips. One evergreen (a spruce or pine) provides a mass of dark tone in the mid-left background. The ground is cold, damp earth — not muddy, but dark with moisture. Small puddles in the uneven spots reflect the sky.

Near the base of the Bauwagen, a cluster of broken terracotta flower pots. Among and between them, small green shoots of hornveilchen (horned violets) are pushing up — tiny, brave, barely there but unmistakably alive. The first sign of spring on the yard.

Further back, half-hidden by the scrap piles, the peaked roof of the toolshed is visible. Its door is closed but slightly ajar — inviting future exploration.

## Background Stories

- On a distant scrap pile (far right background), a crow is perched, silhouetted against the cloudy sky. Just sitting. Watching. It noticed Konsti arrive but isn't overly interested. One background story begins.
- A small puddle near the broken flower pots has a single dead leaf floating in it — and if you look closely, a tiny ripple where a drop just fell from the Bauwagen's gutter.
- A loose sheet of corrugated metal leaning against a junk pile is caught mid-vibration by the wind — its edge slightly blurred, suggesting movement.
- On the Bauwagen itself, near the window frame, a small spider has begun a web between the frame and a nail. The web is only half-built — a work in progress. The spider is tiny but visible.

## Mood

Quiet joy. Arrival. The feeling of a small creature encountering the exact right place and knowing it, even though he couldn't have known he was looking for it. The atmosphere is gentle and safe — a children's book feeling. The partly cloudy sky gives the scene a contemplative quality: not dramatic, not bleak, just the honest grey-and-pale-blue of a March afternoon in Germany. There is wind but it is not harsh. There is damp but it is not cold. Everything says: *this is the beginning of something.*

## Art Style & Technical Requirements

- High-resolution pixel art in Studio Ghibli style — "256-bit, not 8-bit." Every pixel is deliberate. The image is dense with detail and atmosphere. Think of the textured, warm world of Ghibli — worn wood grain, fogged glass, beaded water droplets, the thousand tiny marks of a lived-in place — rendered in meticulous pixel art.
- **Grayscale only** — the full range of 256 shades from deep black to clean white. No color whatsoever. Contrast, texture, and tonal depth do the work of color. Rich midtones for the Bauwagen wood, deep blacks in the window interior and shadows, bright highlights on wet surfaces and sky breaks.
- **800×600 pixels, landscape orientation (4:3).** Image fills the frame edge to edge. No borders, no frame.
- **No text, no speech bubbles, no captions, no letters.** Not a single character of text anywhere in the image.
- **No accessories on Konsti.** He is just himself — a small, fluffy, round young owl.
- **Rich environmental detail always.** The Schrottplatz world is alive in every corner. No plain backgrounds.
- **Konsti is the emotional center and focal point** — positioned in the lower-center of the composition on the windowsill, with the large window and the world of the yard framing him.

**A reference image of Konsti is attached and should be used to maintain visual consistency for the character's appearance.** Match his proportions, fluffiness, eye size, and ear tuft shape from the reference.

## Continuity Notes

This is the very first image in the series. There is no prior continuity to maintain. This image *establishes* continuity:
- The Bauwagen window's appearance (size, frame, condensation patterns)
- The windowsill's character (worn wood, width)
- The arrangement of the scrapyard visible from this angle
- The location of the broken flower pots and hornveilchen
- The toolshed's position relative to the Bauwagen
- The crow on the distant scrap pile (may or may not recur)
- The spider's web-in-progress on the window frame

All of these elements are now canonical and should be referenced in future images for consistency.
