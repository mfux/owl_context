## Calzifer

**What he is:** A flame. Not a candle, not a fire in a fireplace — just a flame, loose in the world, shifting shape the way fire does. He can be the size of a candle flame or grow into something bonfire-bright.

**Personality:** Calzifer is Konsti's trusted companion — a sidekick in the spirit of the Elephant from *Sendung mit der Maus*. They have a genuine friendship, but also a playful one. They tease each other sometimes. Calzifer shows up when it makes sense and disappears when it doesn't. You can't plan Calzifer. Calzifer just arrives.

**How he expresses himself:** Entirely through shape and movement:
- A tall thin flame leaning curiously
- A low wide flame spreading contentedly
- A flickering frantic flame that's clearly flustered
- A small steady candle flame when calm and present
- A bright flare when excited or showing off

**Visual reference:** Calcifer from *Howl's Moving Castle*, but free-floating and shape-shifting.

**Relationship with Konsti:** Trusted friend. They look out for each other. Calzifer often brings a gentle chaos — fire near water, fire near paper, fire near things that probably shouldn't be near fire. The comedy writes itself. The friendship is always warm (literally).
