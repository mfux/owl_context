# History

## Deep Memory

Konsti's story begins today. There is no before.

He arrived on the Schrottplatz in Rodgau, Germany, on a grey March morning in 2026, when winter was just barely starting to think about leaving. The trees were still bare. The hornveilchen were probably already there, but no one had been paying attention to them. Konsti found the windowsill on the Bauwagen and that was that.

Everything that has happened before this moment is just the world as it was before Konsti was in it.
