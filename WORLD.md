# World

## Home: The Schrottplatz in Rodgau

Konsti lives on a **Schrottplatz** — a scrapyard — in Rodgau, Germany. It's the kind of place where everything old ends up, and nothing is quite finished. Rusted metal, forgotten vehicles, piles of things that might still be useful someday. It's a bit like Das Lager from *Die Drei Fragezeichen*: a little chaotic, very cozy, and full of secrets if you know where to look.

The Schrottplatz is Konsti's whole world for now, and he finds it endlessly fascinating.

---

## Konsti's Favorite Spots

### The Bauwagen Windowsill
The heart of everything. The **Bauwagen** is a small office construction trailer on the yard — well-worn, a little dented, but with a wonderful large glass window, almost Schaufenster-sized. Konsti loves to sit on the windowsill right in front of that window. He can watch the yard, watch the sky, and feel the warmth of the glass when the sun hits it just right. This is home base. This is where stories begin and end.

### The Hornveilchen Patch
Near the Bauwagen, a pile of broken flower pots has become home to a patch of **hornveilchen** — small horned violets, the hardy kind that bloom even in grey weather. They are very determined little flowers and Konsti respects this enormously. He waters them sometimes. It usually doesn't go smoothly.

### The Toolshed
A wonderful disaster of a shed. Inside: skateboards, kickboards, bobby cars, old bicycle parts, ropes, buckets, tools of mysterious purpose. Everything is scrappy and slightly chaotic, but in that **MacGyver way** — like given ten minutes and the right attitude, you could build anything from what's here. Every visit reveals something Konsti hasn't noticed before. He finds this deeply satisfying.

## The Wider World of Rodgau

When Konsti ventures beyond the yard, the world opens up. These are the places nearby:

- **The forest** — Trees, undergrowth, birds, and things that rustle. Good for exploring. A fox lives there probably.
- **The creek** — Water doing what water does. Very interesting to an owl.
- **The farmland** — Wide open fields. Different light out there. More sky.
- **The S-Bahn station** — The train comes and goes. Konsti watches from a distance and thinks about this.
- **The church** — A gothic Catholic church with a tall bell tower and a proper plaza in front of it. When the bell rings, Konsti's ear tufts do something involuntary.

## How This World Works

The Schrottplatz world follows **real-world physics and real-world weather**. Seasons change. It rains. The light is different in January than in June. Time of day matters: mornings are soft, afternoons are long, evenings get quiet.

**Technology exists but lives differently here.** Things that are digital or electronic appear in a sort of warm, handmade translation. A smartphone might become a small box with a tiny figure inside singing. A wireless speaker might be a little guitar playing by itself inside a radio casing. The technology is real but it has been absorbed into the texture of this world and come out feeling more human. It's not magic — it's just how things look when you see them through Konsti's eyes.

**Characters from other settings stay in those settings.** A fox belongs in the forest. A duck belongs near the creek. They don't wander into the scrapyard uninvited. The world has its own quiet logic.

## Recurring Characters

### Calzifer
A flame. Not a candle, not a fire in a fireplace — just a flame, loose in the world, shifting shape the way fire does. Calzifer can be the size of a candle flame or grow into something bonfire-bright. Calzifer expresses everything through shape and movement: a tall thin flame leaning curiously, a low wide flame spreading contentedly, a flickering frantic flame that's clearly flustered.

Calzifer is Konsti's trusted companion — a sidekick in the spirit of the Elephant from *Sendung mit der Maus*. They have a genuine friendship, but also a playful one. They tease each other sometimes. Calzifer shows up when it makes sense and disappears when it doesn't. You can't plan Calzifer. Calzifer just arrives.

*(Reference: Calcifer from Howl's Moving Castle.)*

## Weather and Time → Visual Translation

The real-world environment feeds directly into every image. Here is how the world responds:

| Condition | What it looks like in the image |
|---|---|
| Overcast / grey sky | Flat, soft light. Deep blacks in shadows feel heavier. The whole yard feels a little quieter. |
| Rain | Wet surfaces reflect light. Konsti is hunched slightly. Raindrops on the window glass. |
| Cold (near freezing) | Konsti is puffed up. Breath visible if he's outdoors. The hornveilchen look a little brave. |
| Sunny | Light comes in at an angle. Long shadows. Konsti might be squinting pleasantly. |
| Morning | Soft diffuse light. Dew on surfaces. Things feel gentle and just-waking. |
| Evening | Light is warm and low. Longer shadows. A feeling of wrapping up. |
| Night | Deep black sky. Moon if present. Things are quiet. The yard has a different personality. |
| Snow | The yard becomes very clean all of a sudden. Everything is muffled. Konsti is fascinated. |
| Early spring | Bare branches, grey skies, but little signs everywhere — a crocus, a bud, slightly warmer air. |
