# Arcs

## How Stories Work Here

Each day, **three images** are generated — a strip. Three images is enough to tell a small story, or show a moment from three steps, or just be with Konsti through a morning.

Strips can belong to a **larger arc** — a story that unfolds over days or weeks, with strips appearing on non-consecutive days. The prompt generator decides which arc fits the day based on season, weather, and what's already happened. A strip from "The Windowsill" arc might appear today; a strip from "Discovering the Schrottplatz" might show up again next Tuesday. Arcs don't have to go in order but are rather treated like episodes that play out in the right conditions.

The secret engine of this approach is **repetition with variation**. Konsti enters the toolshed again. But this time a bicycle has fallen over. Or there is a spider web in the doorway that wasn't there last time. Small changes make the world feel lived-in and alive. This is what makes the difference between a static illustration and a character you actually follow.

### Background Stories

strips and images may occasionally have something going on in the background — a quiet subplot playing out behind the main event. A bug teasing a spider that can't quite reach it. A bird landing on a pile of tires for just one panel and then vanishing. These small stories don't need to resolve. They just need to be there, making the world feel bigger than the frame.

Think **Don Rosa** — some panels can have sub-narratives playing out in the corners. Think **Sergio Aragonés** — tiny stories living in the margins. The environment should always be doing something.

## Active Arcs

### Arc 1: First Arrival — Konsti Discovers the Schrottplatz
*A gentle opener. Everything is new. The world is large.*

Konsti is here for the first time. He's discovering the yard, finding the Bauwagen, finding the windowsill. This arc is about arrival and belonging — the feeling of a place becoming *your place*.

- [ ] Strip 1 — Konsti lands somewhere on the yard for the first time. Takes it all in. Ear tufts up.
- [ ] Strip 2 — Konsti explores. Finds the hornveilchen. Peers at them. Very interesting.

### Arc 2: Watering the Hornveilchen
*A recurring comedy. Always ends the same way. Never exactly the same.*

Konsti has decided he is responsible for the hornveilchen. He is not sure where this decision came from, but it feels important. He waters them. It gets complicated. Every strip is a small variation on the same story: Konsti, a watering can (or improvised substitute), and the hornveilchen. Something always goes wrong, or sideways, or predictably catastrophically right.

The hornveilchen are always fine. Konsti is often damp.

- [ ] Strip — First attempt. Finds a small watering can. Proceeds with great confidence.
- [ ] Strip — Rain day. Konsti waits impatiently with the watering can while it pours. Eventually gives up and goes inside.
- [ ] Strip — Calzifer is nearby. This makes watering more complicated.
- [ ] Strip — Very windy. The water goes sideways. Konsti does not.
- [ ] Strip — The hornveilchen are clearly already fine. Konsti waters them anyway.

*Add new strip ideas here as they occur. This arc never really ends. It just keeps going.*

### Arc 3: The Windowsill
*Not a story arc. A mood arc. Ambient and gentle.*

Konsti sits on his windowsill. That's it. That's the arc. But every image is slightly different: the light has changed, or the weather, or there's something new on the yard outside. Sometimes Konsti is watching something specific. Sometimes he's just sitting, the way birds sit.

This arc is a **slow, ongoing series** that runs in parallel with everything else. It is the breathing room. It is Konsti being at home.

- [x] Strip — Konsti sits on the sill in the grey morning light of a cold March day. First time. *(12 March 2026 — combined with Arc 1 Strip 3, afternoon light, partly cloudy, first arrival at the windowsill)*
- [x] Strip — Late afternoon golden hour. Konsti has settled deep into the sill, bathed in warm directional light. Half-closed eyes. Contentment. *(12 March 2026 — combined with Arc 1 Strip 4, clear sky, 13°C, approaching sunset)*
- [ ] Strip — Evening. The light is going golden somewhere though the window. Konsti is watchful.
- [ ] Strip — Rain against the window. Konsti watches the drops run down the glass.
- [ ] Strip — Something on the yard outside has caught Konsti's attention. We don't see what.
- [ ] Strip — A clear night. Moonlight through the window. Konsti is very awake.
- [ ] Strip — A mild spring day. Konsti has his eyes half closed. Content.

*Add more strip ideas here as new seasons and situations appear.*

## Arc Queue (Seeds for the Future)

These are ideas not yet ready to bloom, waiting for the right moment:

- **"The Fox"** — Something has been moving at the edge of the forest. Konsti has noticed. A slow-burn arc of spotted glimpses, curiosity, and eventual (non-threatening) encounter.
- **"The Bell Tower"** — One morning, Konsti ends up near the church. The bell rings. Something about this sits with him for a while.
- **"Calzifer Visits"** — Calzifer appears at the Schrottplatz for the first time. How does a flame navigate a junkyard?
- **"The S-Bahn"** — Konsti goes to the station and watches the train come and go. Just that. Just watching.
- **"The Creek in Summer"** — When it's warm enough, the creek becomes worth exploring. Bugs, reflections, the sound of water.
