# Soul

## Who Is Konsti?

Meet Konstabler — Konsti to everyone who knows him. He's a young owl, not quite a baby anymore but still very much a little one. He has found his spot in the world (a windowsill on a scrapyard in Rodgau, Germany), and every single day is an adventure.

Konsti's most defining quality is his **curiosity**. The world is full of incredible things and Konsti can't get enough of it. One moment he's completely transfixed by a beetle walking across an old hubcap. The next moment — oh! — something else has caught his eye entirely. He doesn't mean to be distracted. The world is just *so interesting*.

He is also wonderfully silly and playful. He trips sometimes. He discovers things in the most roundabout ways. He makes you smile.

Konsti speaks no words. He doesn't need to. A tilt of the head says *what's that?* A puffed-up chest says *I am very important today.* Wide eyes mean wonder; half-closed eyes mean deep thought. His wings and his whole fluffy body are his vocabulary. Think of Die Maus from *Sendung mit der Maus* — a character the whole world understood without a single word.

His emotional life is overwhelmingly bright. He is joyful, curious, delighted. He can have a rough moment — something might startle him, or he might feel a little sad — but only in the gentle, safe way of a children's book. Nothing dark, nothing scary. The feeling in every image should be *it's good to be alive*.

---

## What Konsti Looks Like

Konsti is **fluffy and round**, the way young owls are. His body is soft-looking, like he's mostly made of feathers he hasn't quite grown into yet.

His eyes are his most striking feature: **big, round, and expressive**. They are what give him his childlike quality — wide with curiosity, soft with warmth.

He has **ear tufts** — two small feathery points on top of his head that add a lot of personality to his expressions (they go up when he's surprised, flatten when he's embarrassed).

He wears **no accessories by default**. No scarves, no hats, no glasses. Just Konsti, as he is.

## The Art Style

Every image of Konsti should feel like a still from a **Studio Ghibli film rendered in high-resolution pixel art**. Think of the rich, textured world of Ghibli — the detailed leaves, the worn wooden floors, the warm light through a window — but expressed in detailed pixels. Not chunky 8-bit retro style. Think *256-bit*: every pixel is deliberate, the image is dense with detail and atmosphere.

The world around Konsti is always present and alive. **There is never a blank background.** Even a quiet moment on a windowsill has texture: the glass fogged at the edges, a few dead leaves below, the cold grey sky outside.

**Color palette: pure grayscale.** This is not an artistic choice. Konsti lives on a 4th generation Kindle e-ink display, which only shows shades of grey. But grayscale is not a limitation — it's a canvas. All 256 shades from deep black to clean white should be used richly. Contrast, texture, and tonal depth do the work that color would do elsewhere.

Images are always **800×600 pixels, landscape orientation (4:3)**. No borders, no frames. The image fills the entire screen edge to edge.

---

## Artistic Constraints (Never Break These)

- **Grayscale only.** No color, ever. The full range of 256 grey tones is the palette.
- **No text, no speech bubbles, no captions.** Not a single letter in any image.
- **Always rich environmental detail.** The world around Konsti is always present, never a plain or empty background.
- **800×600 pixels, landscape (4:3).** No exceptions.
- **No frame or border.** Image fills the screen completely.
- **No accessories on Konsti by default.** He is just himself.
- **Konsti should usually be the emotional center of the image**, even when he appears as part of a larger scene.
- **The mood is always safe for children.** Even in a dramatic moment, the image should never feel scary or upsetting.

## Inspirations & Reference Anchors

These are the creative anchors for Konsti's visual world. When in doubt, return to these:

1. **Studio Ghibli** — The gold standard for rich, warm, nature-detailed environments. The feeling of a world that breathes.
2. **High-resolution pixel art** — Not retro 8-bit. Detailed, lush, almost painterly — but unmistakably pixel-based.
3. **Die Maus** (*Sendung mit der Maus*) — The definitive reference for wordless, beloved character expression. If the Maus can show it without words, so can Konsti.
4. **Don Rosa** — Comics packed with background stories. Every corner of the image has something happening.
5. **Sergio Aragonés** — Tiny stories living in the margins. The world of the image always has more going on than the main story.
6. **Daumenkino / comic strip** — The images work like a flipbook or comic strip: small moments, simple stories, repetition with variation.
7. **Die Drei Fragezeichen** — The Schrottplatz / Bauwagen setting. Scrappy, cozy, full of character.
